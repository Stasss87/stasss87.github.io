Google Drive connection

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 August 2026

Scope

This notice describes the Google Drive delivery connection in UCC MCA Pipeline. It covers how that connection handles Google account access and delivery files. The office operator is responsible for the collection, screening, retention and permitted use of the underlying lead database.

Information used

The application receives OAuth access and refresh tokens after the account owner grants permission through Google. It uses the drive.file scope to work with files it creates and items selected for it through Google Picker. The delivery folder is selected and approved by the account owner.

The connection checks folder and file metadata, such as IDs, names, permissions, sizes and checksums. It uploads the accepted daily CSV and Excel files. Those files may contain business and contact information, including names, telephone numbers and screening indicators. The connection does not request access to Gmail, Google Contacts or unrelated Drive files.

Use and sharing

Google account access is used to validate the selected folder, upload the daily files, verify their integrity and avoid duplicate uploads. Files are transferred directly from the office computer to Google Drive over HTTPS. They become available to people with access to the destination folder.

The connection does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. It does not add sharing permissions. The office operator controls who receives folder access and any additional copies.

Where operational monitoring is enabled, aggregate results such as upload status, dates, file sizes, checksums and error codes can be sent to the configured project monitoring channel. Those reports do not contain OAuth tokens, Google folder IDs, lead names, telephone numbers or file contents. They are available to people authorized to access that monitoring channel.

Storage and removal

The connection stores authorization credentials and upload state on the office computer. On Windows, saved authorization credentials are encrypted for the Windows user running the scheduled application. Local setup files must also be kept private. Google stores uploaded files in the account's Drive under its service terms and privacy policy.

Credentials and upload state remain locally until removed. Uploaded files remain in Google Drive until the account owner or an authorized user removes them; the connection does not automatically delete uploaded files. Turning off uploads does not revoke Google access or erase existing copies.

The account owner can revoke the application's access in their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Contact support for assistance disabling uploads and removing local credentials. Copies already downloaded by colleagues must be managed separately by the office operator.

This information website

These static pages have no sign-in form, advertising or analytics scripts. They do not receive Google authorization tokens or delivery files. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
