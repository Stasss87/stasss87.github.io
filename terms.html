Google Drive connection

Terms of Use

Effective 28 August 2026

Authorized office use

The Google Drive connection is an optional part of UCC MCA Pipeline for the configured office. Authorize only a Google account you control and select a delivery folder you are allowed to use. The account owner remains responsible for storage, folder sharing and the permitted use of the files.

Only authorized colleagues should receive delivery-folder access. Do not distribute setup credentials or authorization tokens with the lead files. The connection is not a public sign-up service, and these information pages do not provide access to the office application or any customer database.

Delivery and calling

The application uploads accepted daily CSV and Excel snapshots, not a live shared database. Daily files may overlap. Keep call outcomes separately and do not treat a repeated lead in a later file as a new permission to call.

Google Drive delivery neither screens telephone numbers nor establishes legal permission to call them. Follow the office's screening and calling requirements before using any telephone number.

Availability and disconnection

Delivery depends on the office computer, scheduled tasks, network access, Google authorization, storage and API availability. A missing or failed upload must be checked; silence does not establish successful delivery. The local accepted files remain available when a cloud upload fails.

The account owner may revoke Google access. Support can help disable uploads. Disconnection does not delete files already uploaded or copies already downloaded by colleagues.
